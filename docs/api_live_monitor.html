What this page can and cannot see. It reports only the traffic it generates — its own health poll, its WebSockets, and the probes you click. It is not a proxy or a tap, so calls made by the main UI at :5174 in another tab do not appear here. To watch a specific interaction, reproduce it with the probes above.

Timeout budgets come from hive_api_gateway/app/config.py: /tasks 8s · /nav_goal 6s · /cancel_nav 5s · /webrtc/offer 6s. The robot-side bridge answers within its own 4.5s budget, so a bar that reaches the dashed line means the robot never replied — that is the 504 path.
